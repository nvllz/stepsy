<p><b>Stepsy</b> is perfect for anyone who wants a simple, private way to track steps. It’s a lightweight, battery-friendly Android app that uses your phone’s built-in sensors. It prioritizes simplicity, privacy, and full offline data storage.</p><p>Stay active, monitor your progress with flexible reports, and hit your fitness goals with negligible battery impact.</p><ul><li>Estimate distance and calories burned based on your height and weight</li><li>View your activity trends with dynamic, interactive charts</li><li>Export your data as a JSON or CSV for analysis</li><li>Use the home screen and lockscreen widgets to keep your step count front and center</li><li>Tweak the app to make use of it based on your needs</li></ul><p>Stepsy never connects to the Internet. This FOSS version doesn't include the Activity Recognition API, meaning step filtering is less accurate. If you have Google Play Services anyway, it's worth it to rely on Obtainium for updates and use the full build instead.</p>
